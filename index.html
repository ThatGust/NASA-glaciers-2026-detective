                Moon case

                🔍 Your guess:

                  How do we test it?

                  1. The Moon's 18.6-year rhythm. The Moon's tilted orbit slowly turns. At a "major standstill" (2006, 2025) its pull swings most; at a "minor standstill" (2015) least. The dashed curve shows it.
                  2. Remove the long-term trend. Otherwise warming could be mistaken for the Moon.
                  3. Compare with the Moon's curve. Do the leftover ups and downs follow it?
                  4. Test against placebos. We make 999 fake records with the same year-to-year memory but no Moon. If fewer than 5 in 100 match the curve as well, the signal is "Moon-like". Even then, it is not proof: other climate rhythms last about 20 years too.

                      Projection · What comes next?

                      How does the model predict?

                      1. One value per year. Averaging each year cancels the seasons, so only the slow change is left.
                      2. Draw the trend. Sen's slope takes the middle of all year-to-year slopes, so one odd year cannot bend it.
                      3. Test it on the past.
                      4. Extend it, with a margin. The band shows where about 95 of 100 future years should fall if the trend and its usual ups and downs continue. It is a projection, not a prophecy: eruptions, El Niño or human choices can change the path.
