## Lane 5B: Wikimedia Commons place photographs

Photographs in `img/photos/` are contextual illustrations from Wikimedia Commons, separate from the app's NASA scientific series. They retain their source licenses or public-domain status and are not covered by the project's Apache 2.0 license. The per-file source, original file title, plain-text creator, chosen license, license URL and ready-to-display credit are preserved in [data/place_photos.json](../data/place_photos.json). Follow the Commons file page for the full provenance and any additional notices.

- Accept only public-domain works, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA (including versioned jurisdiction/IGO variants). GFDL-only, FAL-only, noncommercial, no-derivatives, fair-use, unspecified and other licenses are excluded. Licensing is checked against Commons imageinfo extmetadata; there is no inference of permission from Wikipedia use alone.
- Whenever a photo is displayed, show its `attribution` text, link its title to the Commons `page`, and link its license name to `license_url` when supplied. The one-line credit is `Photo: <author>, <license>, via Wikimedia Commons`. Keep the manifest with redistributed copies. Credits and source links must remain available in mobile and accessible layouts too.
- These copies were resized to 480 pixels wide and re-encoded as JPEG (at most 40,000 bytes), with orientation applied and transparency flattened onto white if necessary. Identify this processing alongside the credits, for example: "Resized and recompressed from the original." No image-generation model was used. Historical photographs illustrate a place; they are not current observations or evidence of a calculated trend.
- Retain CC BY attribution and the license link. Retain the same CC BY-SA license on adapted photographic copies; the collection does not relicense those images under Apache 2.0. Public-domain and CC0 files still receive source and creator credits as project policy. Do not imply endorsement by a creator, Wikimedia or NASA.

Sources: [Commons reuse guidance](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Reusing_content_outside_Wikimedia), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), [Wikidata data access/CC0](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Data_access).
